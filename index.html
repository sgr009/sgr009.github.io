About

Engineering the infrastructure and tools that scale networks.

I build the systems that turn complex network data into fast, actionable decisions.

After nearly two years in the Network Operations Center, optimizing live network operations, I moved into Network Planning & Design to design infrastructure and the tools behind smarter network decisions.

Today, I develop production-grade Streamlit platforms for feasibility analysis, geospatial intelligence, link monitoring, and workflow automation, while also architecting scalable Proxmox VE environments and virtual infrastructure.

My experience spans National Transmission Network (NTTN), International Internet Gateway (IIG) operations, and related carrier-grade infrastructure, giving me hands-on insight into how data flows across backbone, metro, and cross-border networks. This deep understanding of physical and logical layers informs every tool I build.

My focus is simple: build systems that make complex work feel effortless.

The result: solutions that have reduced manual reporting by 75% and helped teams move faster with confidence.
